06 — THE LONG GAME

Mana is tempo

Your best pace is the one that gets you to the next mob with enough fuel to finish the pull.

  • 01Use the free cast. Arcane Concentration procs are a natural cue to spend on Arcane Missiles.
  • 02Stop before empty. A short wand window can cost less time than a full drink break.
  • 03Drink between pulls. Conjured water keeps recovery close; sit down while looting or travelling.
  • 04Use consumables deliberately. Save mana potions for hard fights or when uptime matters.

08 — PACK YOUR BAGS

Small supplies, smoother pulls

Carry practical consumables; use them when they save real downtime or stabilize a hard fight.

Mana Potions

Use on a difficult pull or when continuing is faster than drinking. Keep one in an accessible bag slot.

Mageweave & food buffs

Use Mageweave bandages for emergency recovery if trained; food buffs are a low-effort boost before longer sessions.

Dark Runes

Optional emergency mana restoration, typically costly and limited. Save for a dangerous situation, not routine pulls.

09 — FIELDCRAFT

Tips & tricks

Simple habits for steady experience, fewer corpse runs and less time spent drinking.

01 / MANA

Manage the bar, not the meter

Do not force a full burst every pull. Use your wand or lower-cost casts when safe, drink while looting, and save potions for meaningful pressure.

02 / PULLS

Start small, then scale

One controlled target is the default. Add enemies only when you have space, cooldowns and an escape route; avoid fighting near respawns.

03 / AOE

AoE has rules

Group enemies deliberately and check the surroundings first. Arcane Explosion is close-range; use slows, Frost Nova and Blink to manage danger, not to justify an oversized pull.

04 / QUESTS & GOLD

Follow efficient loops

Stack nearby quests, sell vendor trash, keep bags clear, and avoid long detours for low-value objectives. Learn flight paths and keep a little gold for training and water.

10 — QUICK ANSWERS

Before you head out

A few answers to keep your leveling route flexible.

Q 01Is Arcane a good leveling specialization?+

It can be a strong and satisfying path, with useful burst and efficient Clearcasting windows. Your comfort with mana pacing matters as much as the build; compare server-specific tuning and choose the rhythm you enjoy.

Q 02Should I use Arcane Blast on every cast?+

No need to tunnel on stacks. Arcane Blast gets more expensive as you build its effect, so let your mana pool, target health, and Clearcasting procs decide when to continue and when to release or reset.

Q 03What do I do when I run low on mana?+

Pause the expensive burst, use a wand where practical, then drink between pulls. Conjured water and mana potions help smooth recovery, but neither replaces sensible pull pacing.

Q 04Are the talent points and spell levels exact for Forever?+

Treat this as a leveling framework rather than a ruleset guarantee. Server patches may alter availability, balance, and rank acquisition. Check your in-game trainer and current server resources as you level.
