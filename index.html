Leveling Path

Rotation

  1. Build Arcane Blast up to 4 stacks (don't force 4 blindly — procs come first).
  2. Cast Arcane Missiles whenever Clearcasting procs.
  3. Spend stacks with Arcane Barrage.
  4. Use Presence of Mind + instant cast for burst windows.
  5. Drink / use mana potions early enough — never sit at 0 mana mid-fight.

Talent Tree (Arcane, 51 points)

Standard leveling build: full Arcane tree first, then Frost for Improved Frostbolt / utility. Key picks by tier:

Tier 1 · 5 ptsArcane Subtlety (5/5) → Arcane Focus (5/5)
Tier 2 · 10 ptsImproved Arcane Missiles (5/5)
Tier 3 · 15 ptsArcane Concentration (5/5) — Clearcasting procs
Tier 4 · 20 ptsMagic Attunement (2/2) → Improved Arcane Explosion (3/3)
Tier 5 · 25 ptsArcane Resilience (1/1)
Tier 6 · 30 ptsPresence of Mind — instant next spell
Tier 7 · 35 ptsArcane Meditation (3/3) — mana regen while casting
Tier 8 · 40 ptsImproved Blink (2/2)
Tier 9 · 45 ptsSpell Power (2/2)
Tier 11 · 51 ptsArcane Power — capstone, +30% damage / -30% cost

Remaining points after Arcane: Frost tree for Improved Frostbolt (faster cast) and Ice Block / Frost Nova utility.

Gear Priorities

  • Intellect > Spirit > Stamina. Spell power and crit are secondary.
  • Weapon: highest DPS staff/wand available — early on a weapon is stronger than a cheap wand.
  • Don't over-invest in gear while leveling; quest rewards and dungeon drops are enough.

Mana Management

  • Evocation / mana potions between pulls, not during.
  • Arcane Meditation lets you regen while casting — keep casting.
  • Skip Arcane Explosion on single targets to save mana.

FAQ

Arcane or Frost for leveling?

Arcane has the highest single-target burst with proper mana management; Frost is safer for solo play. This guide follows the Arcane path.

Why not 4 Arcane Blast stacks every time?

Clearcasting procs and cooldowns matter more than raw stacks — spending on Arcane Missiles/Barrage at the right moment is more damage.

Does this work in dungeons?

Yes — same rotation, just manage mana so the healer isn't drinking constantly.
